★ Coordination Compounds
► Introduction and nomenclature
◆ **Coordination entity:** A central metal atom or ion surrounded by ligands, such as [Fe(CN)6]3-.
◆ **Ligand:** An ion or molecule that donates an electron pair to the central metal ion.
  • Examples include H2O, NH3 and Cl-.
◆ **Coordination number:** The number of donor atoms attached to the central metal.
::: keypoint याद रखें
A ligand may be monodentate, bidentate or polydentate. दाता परमाणुओं की संख्या पर ध्यान दें।
:::
► Crystal field splitting
The five d orbitals split into two sets in an octahedral field. The energy difference is represented by Δo.
[eq] \mu_s = \sqrt{n(n+2)} [/eq]
::: example Magnetic moment
**Question:** Find the spin-only magnetic moment for three unpaired electrons.
**Solution:** μs = √(3(3+2)) = √15 ≈ 3.87 BM.
:::
